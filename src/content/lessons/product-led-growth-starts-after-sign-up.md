## Free isn’t the strategy

Every few quarters someone asks why we don’t “just go product-led”. What they usually mean is a free plan and a sign-up button. That’s **acquisition**, and it’s the easy part.

Product-led growth is a bet that the product does the selling. The bet only pays off if people *feel* the value before anyone talks to them. A free plan that leads nowhere is just a cheaper way to lose people.

## Find the first moment of value

Before you touch pricing, find the moment a new user thinks “oh, this is useful”. For a payments app it was the first sale taken, not the account created.

1. Write that moment down as one sentence a customer would say.
2. Measure how many new users reach it, and how long it takes.
3. Remove every step between sign-up and that moment that doesn’t earn its place.

> If a new user can’t say what changed for them in the first session, no free plan will save you.

## Build loops, not funnels

A funnel ends: someone signs up, and you buy the next visitor. A loop feeds itself: what one user does brings in the next one. The team at Reforge makes the case well in [Growth Loops are the New Funnels](https://www.reforge.com/blog/growth-loops).

Look for the loop your product already has:

- **Content loops:** what users make is found by people who aren’t users yet.
- **Collaboration loops:** inviting a colleague is part of getting the job done.
- **Paid loops:** revenue from engaged users funds the next round of acquisition.

Pick one and make it faster. Three half-built loops grow slower than one that works.

## Watch the curve, not the count

Sign-ups are the number everyone celebrates and the one that matters least. The number that tells you if product-led growth is working is **retention**: the share of each week’s new users still active weeks later.

Plot it by cohort. If the curve keeps falling, you have a leak, and more acquisition only fills a bucket with a hole in it. If it flattens, you’ve found people who stay, and *engagement* work can start to pay back.

Start with the moment of value this week, then pick your loop. The free plan can wait until both are in place.
